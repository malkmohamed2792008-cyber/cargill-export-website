\# PROJECT REQUIREMENTS



\## Project Overview



Develop a professional corporate website for \*\*CARGILL\*\*, an Egyptian company specializing in the export and import of fresh fruits, vegetables, and frozen food products.



The website is designed to present the company's services, strengthen its brand identity, and generate business inquiries from international clients.



The website is informational only.



It does NOT require:



\- E-commerce

\- User accounts

\- Login system

\- Dashboard

\- Database (Phase 1)

\# About Us



\## Company Story



Founded in 2024, CARGILL is an Egyptian company specializing in the export and import of premium-quality fresh fruits, vegetables, and frozen food products. Our mission is to connect Egyptian agricultural excellence with international markets by providing reliable sourcing, strict quality control, and efficient logistics.



\## Vision



To become one of the leading exporters and importers of fresh produce and frozen foods in the Middle East and global markets through quality, innovation, and long-term partnerships.



\## Mission



To deliver premium agricultural and frozen food products that meet international standards while building lasting relationships based on trust, reliability, and customer satisfaction.
